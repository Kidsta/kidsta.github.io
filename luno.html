At Luno, I led design for the Bundles feature — giving everyday users diversified exposure to crypto without choosing individual coins or timing the market.

The challenge wasn’t the concept, but trust and understanding. Users needed to quickly grasp what a bundle was, how their money was allocated, and why this approach felt safer than buying coins one by one. My focus was shaping a clear, transparent experience that balanced simplicity with the right level of detail.
